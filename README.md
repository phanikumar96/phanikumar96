## 🚀 About Me

I'm an Associate Data Scientist at **Hexaware Technologies** and a Ph.D. researcher in Computer Science & Engineering, specializing in **Generative AI, Machine Learning, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Autonomous Multi-Agent Systems**.

My work focuses on bridging academic research with enterprise AI solutions. I design intelligent agent workflows, scalable Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures, and domain-specific AI applications.

My technical interests include:

- 🤖 Autonomous AI agents and multi-agent orchestration.

- ☁️ Cloud-native AI solutions and enterprise AI automation.

- 🔬 Deep Learning, Computer Vision and applied AI research.
